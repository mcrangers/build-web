    Trail tools

    Two things we built for the trail.

    Icebreaker

    Trail Bingo

    Everyone gets a shuffled 5×5 card and fills squares by finding hikers who match the prompts. It works offline, so it’s fine with no signal.

    Play Trail Bingo →
    • Can name 3 HK hiking trails
    • Is wearing something green
    • Speaks 3 or more languages
    • Studies in a different faculty
    • Free
    • Plays a musical instrument
    • Has a hidden talent (ask!)
    • Is left-handed
    • Took a selfie with you on the trail
